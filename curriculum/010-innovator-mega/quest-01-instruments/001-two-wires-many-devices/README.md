# Mission `001` — Two Wires, Many Devices

[Quest `01` — Instruments](../README.md) · [Innovator Realm (Mega)](../../README.md) · [Curriculum](../../../README.md)

## What the student makes

The Grove distance unit plugged into the Stick, the I²C bus scanned, and every device on it listed by address — including the ones already inside the case

## Concepts and standards

- I²C: two shared wires, one address per device, and `i2c.scan()` as the first thing to try
- Why a bus beats a pin per sensor, and what the four Grove wires actually are
- A module is a sensor with its driver already written
- CSTA 2‑CS‑02, 2‑DA‑07
