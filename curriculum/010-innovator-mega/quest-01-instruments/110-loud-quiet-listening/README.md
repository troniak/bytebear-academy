# Mission `110` — Loud, Quiet, Listening

[Quest `01` — Instruments](../README.md) · [Innovator Realm (Mega)](../../README.md) · [Curriculum](../../../README.md)

## What the student makes

A sound-level meter on the screen driven by the built-in microphone, with an alert the student sets themselves — and an agreed rule about when a microphone in a room may be switched on

## Concepts and standards

- Sampling a microphone; amplitude, averaging over a window, and why loudness is not a single sample
- Calibrating a meter against something known rather than trusting the raw number
- A microphone is a recording device: consent, and what the class agrees not to capture
- CSTA 2‑DA‑08; AI literacy: **data privacy**
