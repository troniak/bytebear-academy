# Mission `111` — Something You'd Carry

[Quest `01` — Instruments](../README.md) · [Innovator Realm (Mega)](../../README.md) · [Curriculum](../../../README.md)

## What the student makes

**Build and show.** An instrument of the student's own — a timer, a level, a noise meter, a distance measure — with a menu, a readable screen and a battery life they can state, demonstrated unplugged

## Concepts and standards

- NGSS practice: **designing solutions and communicating results**
- STEL practice: **making and doing**
- Stating what your device does, how long it lasts, and what it gets wrong

## The unlock

The environment pack, which is what Quest `10` runs on. The student now owns an instrument with a menu, a readable screen and a battery life they can quote — and nothing in particular to point it at. The box has sat sealed on the desk since the post brought it, and comes open on camera with everybody else's. It holds four sensors and the question the next quest opens with: what is the air in your bedroom actually doing while you are asleep in it?
