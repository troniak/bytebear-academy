# Mission `101` — Living on a Battery

[Quest `01` — Instruments](../README.md) · [Innovator Realm (Mega)](../../README.md) · [Curriculum](../../../README.md)

## What the student makes

The Stick's real battery life measured, then doubled — by dimming the screen, reading the sensor less often and sleeping between readings, with each change measured rather than assumed

## Concepts and standards

- A power budget: what a screen costs, what a radio costs, what sleeping saves
- Deep sleep and wake-on-button; duty cycling a sensor
- Measuring your own code's cost, then redesigning because of the number
- CSTA 2‑CS‑01; STEL practice: **systems thinking**
