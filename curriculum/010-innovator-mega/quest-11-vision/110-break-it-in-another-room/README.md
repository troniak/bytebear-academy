# Mission `110` — Break It in Another Room

[Quest `11` — Vision](../README.md) · [Innovator Realm (Mega)](../../README.md) · [Curriculum](../../../README.md)

## What the student makes

Another pod's model deployed onto this pod's own camera, in this pod's room and light and with this pod's objects — every failure logged and sent back to the pod who trained it, one improvement made and measured against the brief, and a model card written

## Concepts and standards

- A model is a file: it travels where the machine cannot, which is what makes this work across sixteen houses
- Being tested by people who did not build it, in conditions you did not choose
- A model card: what it was trained on, where it fails, and who it fails for
- Iteration with evidence rather than with opinion
- CSTA 2‑AP‑17; AI literacy: **Evaluate**
