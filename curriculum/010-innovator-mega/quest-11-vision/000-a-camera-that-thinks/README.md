# Mission `000` — A Camera That Thinks

[Quest `11` — Vision](../README.md) · [Innovator Realm (Mega)](../../README.md) · [Curriculum](../../../README.md)

## What the student makes

The module running a stock model straight out of SenseCraft, drawing boxes around what it recognises in the browser — with the laptop unplugged afterwards to prove the model was never running on it

## Concepts and standards

- Inference at the edge: the model is on the module, not on a laptop and not in a cloud
- Classes, confidence and a box; what a model can and cannot be asked
- Frame rate and inference time as measured quantities
- CSTA 2‑CS‑02; AI literacy: **Understand**

## Materials

- Vision pack — Grove Vision AI V2 module and OV5647 camera, unassembled pan-and-tilt bracket, two micro servos, 4×AA holder as the servo supply
- M5StickS3 from Quest `01` — the one device the build runs on
- Optional second node: the ESP32 from Quest `00`, the sensors from Quest `10`, the Maker rover
- Laptop with a browser for SenseCraft, and Thonny
- Grove-to-jumper conversion cables, and the tested MicroPython driver from the quest folder
