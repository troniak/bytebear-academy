# Mission `100` — The Head That Follows

[Quest `11` — Vision](../README.md) · [Innovator Realm (Mega)](../../README.md) · [Curriculum](../../../README.md)

## What the student makes

The camera on a pan-and-tilt head that keeps its target centred — the box centre turned into an error, the error turned into an angle, and the gain tuned until it tracks without hunting

## Concepts and standards

- Servos: an angle you command, mechanical limits, and their own battery pack sharing a common ground
- Error, gain and proportional correction; damping the oscillation too much gain creates
- A control loop whose timestep is set by how fast the model can see
- CSTA 2‑AP‑17; NGSS core ideas: **forces and motion**; STEL practice: **systems thinking**
