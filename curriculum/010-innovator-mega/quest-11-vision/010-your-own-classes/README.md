# Mission `010` — Your Own Classes

[Quest `11` — Vision](../README.md) · [Innovator Realm (Mega)](../../README.md) · [Curriculum](../../../README.md)

## What the student makes

A model trained on classes the student chose and images they captured themselves, labelled to a written rule, balanced across classes, and deployed back onto the module

## Concepts and standards

- Labelling protocols, class balance, and capture variety as a design decision
- Why the training set decides what the model can ever learn
- Training in the browser and deploying to hardware, without writing the model
- CSTA 2‑DA‑08; AI literacy: **Understand** and **Use**
