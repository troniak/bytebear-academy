# Mission `010` — Numbers That Mean Volts

[Quest `00` — Circuits](../README.md) · [Innovator Realm (Mega)](../../README.md) · [Curriculum](../../../README.md)

## What the student makes

A potentiometer and a light sensor read as raw counts, converted to volts, then mapped into a unit that means something — dark to bright, closed to open

## Concepts and standards

- Analogue input: ADC counts, resolution and reference voltage
- Converting a raw range into a real one, and naming the units
- Voltage dividers, and why a light sensor needs a second resistor
- CSTA 2‑DA‑07; NGSS practice: **planning and carrying out investigations**
