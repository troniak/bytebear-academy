# Mission `100` — Fading, Not Switching

[Quest `00` — Circuits](../README.md) · [Innovator Realm (Mega)](../../README.md) · [Curriculum](../../../README.md)

## What the student makes

An LED that fades rather than switches and a buzzer that holds a pitch, both from the same PWM peripheral with duty cycle and frequency under the student's control

## Concepts and standards

- PWM: duty cycle versus frequency, and why the eye and the ear read them differently
- Ohm's law in practice — sizing a resistor so the LED survives
- Current limits per pin and per board; writing down a power budget
- CSTA 2‑CS‑02; NGSS core idea: **PS3B – energy transfer**
