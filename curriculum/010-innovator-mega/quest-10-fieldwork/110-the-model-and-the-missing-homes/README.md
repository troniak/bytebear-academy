# Mission `110` — The Model and the Missing Homes

[Quest `10` — Fieldwork](../README.md) · [Innovator Realm (Mega)](../../README.md) · [Curriculum](../../../README.md)

## What the student makes

A classifier trained on the pooled class data — window open or shut, room occupied or empty — then run against the one home held out of training, and a written account of whose homes the model was never shown

## Concepts and standards

- Supervised learning on data the students gathered and labelled themselves
- Hold-out testing, and why a single accuracy figure hides the failure
- Who is in a dataset and who is not; what it costs in energy to train at scale
- AI literacy: **Understand** and **Evaluate**; CSTA 2‑DA‑09; STEL practice: **attention to ethics**
