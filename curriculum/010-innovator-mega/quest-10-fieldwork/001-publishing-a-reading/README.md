# Mission `001` — Publishing a Reading

[Quest `10` — Fieldwork](../README.md) · [Innovator Realm (Mega)](../../README.md) · [Curriculum](../../../README.md)

## What the student makes

One reading sent to the class dashboard and appearing on a screen everybody can see, with the student's device named and findable among the others

## Concepts and standards

- Publish and subscribe; a broker as the middle party nobody's device has to be
- Client and server, and why your device needs no address of its own
- CSTA 2‑NI‑04, 2‑CS‑02
