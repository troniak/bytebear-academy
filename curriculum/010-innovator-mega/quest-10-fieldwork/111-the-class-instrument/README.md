# Mission `111` — The Class Instrument

[Quest `10` — Fieldwork](../README.md) · [Innovator Realm (Mega)](../../README.md) · [Curriculum](../../../README.md)

## What the student makes

**Build and show.** Every device live on one dashboard at once, each student presenting their week, their calibration offset, and one way the class dataset does not represent the world

## Concepts and standards

- NGSS practice: **designing solutions and communicating results**
- STEL practice: **collaboration & communication**
- AI literacy: **Evaluate**

## The unlock

The vision pack, which is what Quest `11` runs on. The class has just shown a model trained on numbers gathered from sixteen homes, and named the homes it does not represent. The box, posted ahead and left unopened until now, holds a camera with a neural network processor behind it — the same argument about training data, about to be made again in pictures, where it is much harder to look away from.
