# Mission `101` — A Week in Your Room

[Quest `10` — Fieldwork](../README.md) · [Innovator Realm (Mega)](../../README.md) · [Curriculum](../../../README.md)

## What the student makes

Seven days of air quality, temperature, light and sound from the student's own room — logged unattended by the ESP32 left plugged into the wall for seven days, while the Stick takes spot readings elsewhere — charted against the class, with the human events behind the spikes identified — a window opened, a door shut, a room slept in

## Concepts and standards

- Reading a time series: daily cycles, trends and the events underneath them
- A device you leave running is a different engineering problem from one you hold
- Comparing your home with fifteen others and explaining the difference physically
- What a week of a room reveals about the people in it, and who should be able to see it
- NGSS practice: **analyzing and interpreting data**; AI literacy: **data privacy**
