# Explorer Realm (Byte) — Ages 5+

This stage introduces robotics through story-based missions, expressive builds and simple coding logic. It is designed to make younger children feel that robotics is approachable, friendly and fun while still building real computing foundations. At this realm, the tablet and teammate structure helps young learners participate in guided collaboration without losing the playful tone of the class. For online cohorts, **Microsoft MakeCode** supplements the LEGO work as a browser-only environment that needs no hardware in the home.

## MakeCode as the Online Supplement

Online delivery at this realm uses **Microsoft MakeCode on its own**, in the browser, with no hardware sent to the home. The simulator is the whole platform: children drag the same coloured blocks their classroom peers use, press the run button and see an on-screen device light up, make a sound, respond to a button or react to being tilted. Inputs that would normally need a sensor—tilt, light level, sound, temperature—are sliders and handles on the simulated device, so a five-year-old can still write an if/else and watch it fire.

Keeping the entry realm software-only is deliberate. Families are not asked to buy or assemble anything before they know whether the program suits their child, nothing small or battery-powered has to be supervised at home, a lost or broken device cannot cost a child their session, and a session can start the moment a browser opens on the tablet the student already has. The LEGO builds still anchor each mission, demonstrated live by the instructor on camera so that online students follow the same story and the same concepts as the in-person pods.

Because MakeCode is also the environment used with micro:bit hardware at the Maker realm, the blocks children learn here carry straight over: when they progress, the editor is already familiar and the only new thing is that the program now runs on a device they can hold.
