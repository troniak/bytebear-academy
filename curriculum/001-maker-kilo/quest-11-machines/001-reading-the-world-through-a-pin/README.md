# Mission `001` — Reading the World Through a Pin

[Quest `11` — Machines](../README.md) · [Maker Realm (Kilo)](../../README.md) · [Curriculum](../../../README.md)

## What the student makes

An external analogue sensor read into the micro:bit, its raw numbers calibrated into something meaningful — dry to soaked, dark to bright

## Concepts and standards

- Analogue versus digital
- mapping a raw range onto a real one
- CSTA 2‑DA‑07
- NGSS practice: **planning investigations**
