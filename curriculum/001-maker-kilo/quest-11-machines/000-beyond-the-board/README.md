# Mission `000` — Beyond the Board

[Quest `11` — Machines](../README.md) · [Maker Realm (Kilo)](../../README.md) · [Curriculum](../../../README.md)

## What the student makes

An external LED and buzzer driven from the pins with crocodile clips, and a working explanation of what 3V, GND and a pin actually are

## Concepts and standards

- A pin is an output you wire yourself
- circuits and the one rule about shorting power to ground
- CSTA 2‑CS‑02
