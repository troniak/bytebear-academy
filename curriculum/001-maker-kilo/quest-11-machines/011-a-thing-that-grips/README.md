# Mission `011` — A Thing That Grips

[Quest `11` — Machines](../README.md) · [Maker Realm (Kilo)](../../README.md) · [Curriculum](../../../README.md)

## What the student makes

The gripper or arm assembled and programmed to open, close and hold something real — heavy enough to be hard

## Concepts and standards

- Mechanism, leverage and torque
- the gap between a design that works on paper and one that lifts
- STEL practice: **making and doing**
