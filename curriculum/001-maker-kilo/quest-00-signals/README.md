# Quest `00` — Signals

The micro:bit on its own, for eight missions. There is no rover, no add-on board and nothing to assemble: a student who has the starter pack can do every mission, and a student whose device is at school or in the post can do every mission in the MakeCode simulator and flash it later. Each mission adds one idea to a program the student keeps extending, so by mission `111` they are editing something of their own that is several weeks old rather than starting from a blank workspace.

| Mission | Title | What the student makes | Concepts & standards |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **[`000`](000-hello-bear/)** | [Hello, Bear](000-hello-bear/) | A bear face they draw themselves on the 5x5 grid, shown on start, with their initial scrolling on a button press | A program does nothing until something runs it; `on start` versus `forever`; CSTA 1B‑AP‑10 |
| **[`001`](001-every-input-it-has/)** | [Every Input It Has](001-every-input-it-has/) | A fortune-teller that answers differently to button A, button B, A+B, a shake and the logo touch | Event handlers; one program with many entry points; CSTA 1B‑AP‑10 |
| **[`010`](010-variables-keep-score/)** | [Variables Keep Score](010-variables-keep-score/) | A tally counter that counts shakes, shows the total and resets on A+B | Variables; `set to` versus `change by`; CSTA 2‑AP‑11 |
| **[`011`](011-loops-and-the-grid/)** | [Loops and the Grid](011-loops-and-the-grid/) | A hand-drawn animation on the LED grid using `plot`, `repeat` and `pause` | Definite versus indefinite repetition; x/y coordinates; CSTA 2‑AP‑12 |
| **[`100`](100-if-the-room-changes/)** | [If the Room Changes](100-if-the-room-changes/) | A nightlight and a too-hot alarm driven by the light and temperature sensors | Conditionals over continuous values; choosing and defending a threshold; CSTA 2‑AP‑13 |
| **[`101`](101-naming-what-you-reuse/)** | [Naming What You Reuse](101-naming-what-you-reuse/) | The growing program refactored into named functions, then a planted bug found and fixed | Decomposition and procedures; a systematic debugging routine; CSTA 2‑AP‑14, 1B‑AP‑15 |
| **[`110`](110-an-honest-investigation/)** | [An Honest Investigation](110-an-honest-investigation/) | An overnight log of light, temperature and sound in their own room, charted and compared across the class | micro:bit data logger; CSTA 1B‑DA‑06; NGSS practice: **analyzing and interpreting data**; AI literacy: what a log of a room reveals about the people in it |
| **[`111`](111-pocket-invention/)** | [Pocket Invention](111-pocket-invention/) | **Build and show.** A reaction timer, step counter or dice — chosen by the student, built from the blocks of the previous seven missions, demonstrated live with an explanation of one bug they hit | NGSS practice: **communicating information**; STEL practice: **making and doing** |
