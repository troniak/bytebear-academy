# Mission `111` — Teach the Rover

[Quest `01` — Motion](../README.md) · [Maker Realm (Kilo)](../../README.md) · [Curriculum](../../../README.md)

## What the student makes

**Build and show.** A movement classifier trained on the student's own accelerometer data, wired up to drive the rover, then tested on a classmate who moves differently — and the failure explained

## Concepts and standards

- micro:bit machine-learning tool or Teachable Machine
- AI literacy: **evaluate bias and ethical impact**
