# Mission `110` — Driving It From Across the Room

[Quest `10` — Messages](../README.md) · [Maker Realm (Kilo)](../../README.md) · [Curriculum](../../../README.md)

## What the student makes

The Quest `01` rover put under radio control from the second micro:bit, with a stop command that works even when the rover is out of sight

## Concepts and standards

- Remote control as a protocol, not a wire
- designing a fail-safe
- CSTA 2‑AP‑13. Students without a rover control a classmate's on camera or use a loaner
