## What we'll build

Teams give their program a memory. They start by counting creatures with a single
variable, extend to tracking several at once, then build a pet whose hunger and mood
change as the program runs — debugging the moments where the numbers drift out of step
with the build.

## What your child will learn

- What a variable is: a named box whose contents change while the program runs
- Tracking more than one piece of state without them interfering
- Reading a program's behaviour backwards to find where a value went wrong

## How the session runs

Students work in pods of four with one LEGO kit and one device per pod, rotating
through Designer, Builder, Programmer and Tester-documenter so everyone codes and
everyone builds. The session always ends with a working project and a short demo.

The 4-hour version adds a lunch break and a random-choice and data-analysis challenge,
where teams build something that makes a decision for them and then check whether it's
actually fair.

## What's included

Every pod gets a LEGO Education Computer Science & AI kit and a tablet. This session
builds on conditionals, so it suits students who have already written programs that
make decisions.
