## What we'll build

Teams meet the two kinds of repetition — a loop that runs forever and a loop that runs
a set number of times — and learn to pick the right one. They then debug a misbehaving
waterslide and get several scripts running in coordination.

## What your child will learn

- The difference between a forever loop and a counted loop, and when each is correct
- Spotting the pattern in a program that *should* be a loop
- Coordinating multiple scripts with messages so a build behaves as one machine

## How the session runs

Students work in pods of four with one LEGO kit and one device per pod, rotating
through Designer, Builder, Programmer and Tester-documenter so everyone codes and
everyone builds. The session always ends with a working project and a short demo.

The 4-hour version adds a lunch break and an accessible percussion-performance
challenge, where teams design an instrument that anyone can play.

## What's included

Every pod gets a LEGO Education Computer Science & AI kit and a tablet. Comfort with
sequences and events makes this session smoother, though our instructors bring
newcomers up to speed in the first block.
