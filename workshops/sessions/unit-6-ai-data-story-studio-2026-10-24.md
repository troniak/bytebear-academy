## What we'll build

Teams start with a pose classifier someone else trained, using their own body to steer
a monster through a maze. Then they train one themselves — choosing the examples,
testing it, finding where it fails, and collecting better data until it improves.

## What your child will learn

- What a classifier is: a program that sorts data into categories
- That an AI's behaviour comes from the examples it was trained on, not from rules someone wrote
- How the quality and variety of training data changes accuracy — the most transferable
  idea in the whole sequence

## How the session runs

Students work in pods of four with one LEGO kit and one device per pod, rotating
through story designer, model builder, AI trainer and tester-documenter. The session
always ends with a working project and a short demo.

The 4-hour version adds a lunch break and an interactive storytelling challenge, where
teams collaboratively build training data and use AI poses to tell a story.

## What's included

Every pod gets a LEGO Education Computer Science & AI kit and a tablet. This is the
capstone of the sequence and lands best for students who have already programmed
sequences, events and conditionals.
