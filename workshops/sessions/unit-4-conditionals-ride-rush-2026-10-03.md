## What we'll build

Teams build a colour password that only opens for the right sequence, then move to a
rescue-ship game where the program has to choose between two courses of action
depending on what the sensor sees.

## What your child will learn

- How a condition makes a program branch instead of running straight through
- The difference between `wait until`, `if/then`, and `if/then/else`
- Designing for a user other than yourself, and testing whether it actually works for them

## How the session runs

Students work in pods of four with one LEGO kit and one device per pod, rotating
through Designer, Builder, Programmer and Tester-documenter so everyone codes and
everyone builds. The session always ends with a working project and a short demo.

The 4-hour version adds a lunch break and a user-centred amusement-park design
challenge, where teams design a ride around a rider's needs.

## What's included

Every pod gets a LEGO Education Computer Science & AI kit and a tablet. Students get
most out of this session if they've met sequences, events and loops before — in our
own units or anywhere else.
