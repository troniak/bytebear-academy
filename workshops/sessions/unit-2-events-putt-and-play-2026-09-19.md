## What we'll build

This session is about programs that *wait for something to happen*. Teams start with a
bee that responds to colour messages, then build music machines triggered by key
presses and colour changes — debugging along the way when a trigger fires at the wrong
moment.

## What your child will learn

- What an event is, and how it differs from a plain sequence
- Sending messages between separate scripts so parts of a build stay in sync
- Finding and fixing a bug by isolating which trigger misfired

## How the session runs

Students work in pods of four with one LEGO kit and one device per pod, rotating
through Designer, Builder, Programmer and Tester-documenter so everyone codes and
everyone builds. The session always ends with a working project and a short demo.

The 4-hour version adds a lunch break and an interactive mini-golf challenge, where
teams design a hole that reacts to the ball.

## What's included

Every pod gets a LEGO Education Computer Science & AI kit and a tablet. Unit 1 is
helpful but not required — students who are comfortable with basic sequences can
start here.
